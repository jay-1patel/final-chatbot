# Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: What banks have approved home loans for these projects?
A: Most major banks and housing finance companies offer home loans for Prestige projects, subject to eligibility. The sales team shares the approved‑bank list and sanction details for your chosen project.

### Q: Is the site visit pickup complimentary?
A: Site visits can be scheduled through the menu. Cab pickup availability is confirmed by the sales team at booking.

### Q: What is the booking advance amount?
A: The booking amount differs by project and unit. It is listed in the official cost sheet shared by the sales team; the balance follows the project payment plan.

### Q: Are all projects RERA certified?
A: Prestige projects display their RERA registration numbers, listed against each property in the catalog. Older completed projects may pre‑date RERA.

### Q: Can NRIs purchase units?
A: Yes. NRIs can purchase residential and commercial property in India through NRE/NRO channels, subject to RBI/FEMA rules. Prestige also has an international office in Dubai to assist overseas buyers.

### Q: What is included in the possession handover kit?
A: Handover typically includes keys, occupancy certificate (OC) details, and possession formalities.

### Q: Do you offer construction‑linked payment plans?
A: Payment plans for each project are published in the official cost sheet. Custom plans need sales‑head approval.

### Q: Can I upgrade my unit configuration later?
A: Unit upgrades depend on available inventory and stage of construction. Differential pricing and an agreement addendum may apply.

### Q: What amenities are common across residential projects?
A: Clubhouse, swimming pool, gym, landscaped gardens, security, power backup and parking are typical. Exact lists vary by project.

### Q: How are stamp duty and registration handled?
A: Stamp duty and registration are paid by the buyer as per Karnataka state rates. The documentation team coordinates the appointment and checklist.

### Q: What is your resale/transfer policy before possession?
A: Pre‑possession transfers need builder NOC, a transfer fee as per the agreement, and KYC of the incoming buyer.

### Q: How do I raise a post‑sales complaint?
A: Message your booking ID with the issue, or use **Customer Support** in the menu. You can also call 1800 313 0080 or email properties@prestigeconstructions.com.

### Q: Are floor plans and carpet areas approximate?
A: Marketing floor plans are indicative. Carpet area and final layout follow the RERA‑approved drawings attached to your agreement.

### Q: Do commercial leases include CAM charges?
A: Lease quotes typically exclude CAM, property tax and parking unless stated. Ask for the commercial term sheet for the full break‑up.
