OVERVIEW OF WHAT WAS ACHIEVED:
1. Automated PDF Section Extraction:
   - When a main PDF is uploaded, the system automatically detects the FAQ section
     and Knowledge Base section.
   - It generates two separate small PDFs:
     * `<filename>_faq.pdf` -> placed in the FAQ section.
     * `<filename>_kb.pdf` -> placed in the Knowledge Base section.
   - Internal bot prompt settings (bot persona, guardrails, welcome greetings,
     and parameter rules) as well as internal source conflict tables are automatically
     stripped out from the Knowledge Base PDF so that it contains 100% pure
     customer-facing company knowledge for deep inquiry responses.

2. Strict Tenant Isolation (Admin Panel & WhatsApp Chatbot):
   - Fixed an issue where queries from other tenants (such as "Sbi") appeared under
     Prestige Group (Tenant 14 / Tenant 19).
   - Added automated guards to ensure each tenant only sees and answers their own FAQs.
   - Cross-tenant keywords, switcher commands, and test inputs are automatically
     blocked from being recorded as customer knowledge gaps.

3. Complete UI Redesign of Knowledge Gaps Section:
   - Redesigned the "Top High-Frequency Questions Asked by Customers" card grid.
   - Removed awkward brown/muddy background tints and cramped spacing.
   - Added clean card borders, clear customer count badges, line-clamping, and
     properly aligned "+ 1-Click FAQ" and "Dismiss" buttons.

4. All 5 Properties Catalog & Authentic Image URLs Extraction:
   - Fixed the issue where only 3 corrupted records appeared in the Properties table
     (`/records`) instead of the 5 real properties from the PDF.
   - Eliminated soft hyphens/zero-width space characters (\u200b) and multiline URL breaks.
   - Extracted all 5 tangible real estate projects with complete pricing, configurations,
     locations, and amenities:
     * Prestige Southern Star (Apartment Township, 1-4 BHK, ₹97 Lakhs - ₹3.63 Cr)
     * Prestige Marigold (Gated Villa Plots, 4,000 sq ft, ₹3.52 Cr)
     * Prestige Gardenia Estates (Gated Villa Plots, 1,200 - 4,000 sq ft, ₹1.02 Cr - ₹2.90 Cr)
     * Prestige Thanisandra (Apartments, 2 & 3 BHK, from ₹1.63 Cr & ₹2.12 Cr)
     * Prestige Tech Platina (Commercial Office Grade-A, ₹90 / sq.ft. Lease)
   - Extracted and attached authentic image URLs for all 5 properties (both public WebP/JPG
     links and embedded preview images in /images/catalog/).

5. WhatsApp Menu Buttons Focused Strictly on the PDF:
   - Resolved the issue where menu items were parsed as 29 junk entries or generic defaults.
   - Configured strictly the 6 interactive menu buttons defined under [SECTION: WHATSAPP MENU]:
     1. Explore Properties (2, 3 & 4 BHK luxury residences & villas -> offerings)
     2. Ongoing Projects (Prime locations, masterplans & tower specs -> offering_details)
     3. Schedule Site Visit (Guided property tour with sales advisor -> book_callback)
     4. Price & Payment Plans (Cost sheets, EMI calculations & construction plans -> quote)
     5. RERA & Legal Sanctions (RERA registration, sanctions & bank approvals -> faq)
     6. Customer Support (Existing buyer assistance & documentation help -> raise_ticket)

1. backend/services/pdf_section_extractor.py (NEW FILE)
   ----------------------------------------------------
   - Purpose: Automated extraction and splitting of main uploaded PDFs into
     focused derivative PDFs.
   - What was done:
     * Analyzes PDF pages using font sizes, headings, and keywords to identify
       FAQ pages and Knowledge Base article pages.
     * Generates `<name>_faq.pdf` and registers it under module="faq".
     * Generates `<name>_kb.pdf` and registers it under module="kb".
     * Added `_strip_persona_and_guardrails()`: Automatically redacts and removes
       bot persona tables, tone/style parameters, welcome greetings, prompt guardrails,
       and top conflict/internal tables from the KB PDF so it starts cleanly with articles.
     * Automatically indexes vector search chunks (FAISS) directly from the clean
       derivative files and cleans up the raw multi-megabyte parent file entry.

2. backend/database.py (MODIFIED)
   ------------------------------
   - Purpose: Database operations for knowledge gaps, tenant isolation, and files.
   - What was done:
     * Enhanced `record_unanswered_query()` with cross-tenant validation.
     * If an incoming customer query matches the name, display name, or slug of
       any OTHER tenant (e.g., 'sbi', 'dlf', 'meridianbank'), or is an admin/test
       command (like '#tenant', '#switch'), it is automatically ignored and NOT
       recorded as a knowledge gap for the active tenant.
     * Cleaned up invalid cross-tenant entries from the unanswered queries table.

3. backend/routes/admin.py (MODIFIED)
   ----------------------------------
   - Purpose: Admin API routes for file uploading and tenant management.
   - What was done:
     * Hooked `auto_extract_and_register()` into the document upload background
       task (`_process_document_background`).
     * Whenever an admin uploads a PDF in the Uploads section, the extractor
       automatically creates and registers the FAQ and KB PDFs in their respective
       tabs.

4. backend/services/auto_bifurcator.py (MODIFIED)
   ----------------------------------------------
   - Purpose: Auto-bifurcation pipeline for processing uploaded client files.

       reliably extract all 5 properties and stitch wrapped URLs without corruption.

       the 6 menu buttons defined in the onboarding guide.

5. routing/message.py (MODIFIED)
