**FAQ 1: What is MakeMyTrip?**  
MakeMyTrip is an online travel platform that allows users to book flights, hotels, trains, buses, holiday packages, and other travel services.

**FAQ 2: How can I book a trip on MakeMyTrip?**  
Visit the MakeMyTrip website or app, select your travel service, enter the required details, compare available options, and complete the booking and payment process.

**FAQ 3: Do I need an account to book tickets on MakeMyTrip?**  
Account requirements may vary by service. Creating an account can help you manage bookings, access travel details, and track eligible offers.

**FAQ 4: How can I log in to my MakeMyTrip account?**  
Open the MakeMyTrip website or app and use the available login method, such as your registered mobile number or another supported authentication option.

**FAQ 5: How can I contact MakeMyTrip customer support?**  
Use the Help or My Trips section on the official MakeMyTrip website or app to find support options for your specific booking.

**FAQ 6: Where can I find my booking details?**  
Log in to your account and open the My Trips section. Select the relevant booking to view its itinerary, confirmation details, and other available information.

**FAQ 7: How can I book a flight on MakeMyTrip?**  
Select Flights, enter your departure city, destination, travel dates, and passenger count, then search for flights and complete the booking process.

**FAQ 8: How can I check my flight booking status?**  
Open My Trips, select your flight booking, and review its confirmation and itinerary details. You can also check the airline's official website.

**FAQ 9: Can I cancel my flight ticket on MakeMyTrip?**  
Yes, if cancellation is permitted for your booking. Open My Trips, select the flight, and follow the cancellation instructions. Cancellation charges may apply.

**FAQ 10: Can I change my flight date after booking?**  
Date changes may be possible depending on the airline's fare rules and seat availability. Additional fare differences and change fees may apply.

**FAQ 11: How can I download my flight ticket?**  
Open your confirmed flight booking under My Trips and use the available option to view, download, or print your e-ticket.

**FAQ 12: What should I do if I do not receive my flight confirmation?**  
Check your registered email and SMS messages, then review My Trips. If the booking is still missing, contact customer support with your booking and payment details.

**FAQ 13: How early should I arrive at the airport?**  
Arrival requirements vary by airport and airline. As a general guideline, arrive about two hours before a domestic flight and three hours before an international flight, allowing extra time when needed.

**FAQ 14: Can I add extra baggage to my flight booking?**  
Additional baggage may be available through MakeMyTrip or directly through the airline. Check your fare allowance and the airline's baggage policy before purchasing extra baggage.

**FAQ 15: How can I correct a passenger's name on a flight ticket?**  
Contact MakeMyTrip support and the airline as soon as possible. Minor corrections and full name changes are subject to airline policies and applicable fees.

**FAQ 16: How can I book a hotel on MakeMyTrip?**  
Select Hotels, enter your destination, check-in and check-out dates, and number of guests. Compare available properties and complete your reservation.

**FAQ 17: Can I cancel my hotel booking?**  
Cancellation depends on the property's policy and the rate you selected. Check the cancellation terms in your booking before proceeding.

**FAQ 18: How can I check my hotel reservation confirmation?**  
Open My Trips or your booking confirmation email to view the hotel name, reservation details, guest information, and check-in dates.

**FAQ 19: Can I modify my hotel check-in or check-out dates?**  
Date changes may be possible, depending on room availability and the property's rules. Price differences or modification charges may apply.

**FAQ 20: What does a non-refundable hotel booking mean?**  
A non-refundable booking generally does not qualify for a refund if you cancel. Check the specific booking terms for any applicable exceptions.

**FAQ 21: Is it possible to book a hotel and pay at the property?**  
Some properties offer pay-at-property options, while others require advance payment. The available payment method will be displayed during booking.

**FAQ 22: What should I do if the hotel cannot find my reservation?**  
Show the hotel your booking confirmation and reservation details. If the issue remains unresolved, contact MakeMyTrip support for assistance.

**FAQ 23: Can I request an early check-in or late check-out?**  
You can contact the hotel to request early check-in or late check-out. Approval depends on availability, hotel policy, and any additional charges.

**FAQ 24: How can I book train tickets on MakeMyTrip?**  
Open the Trains section, enter your source station, destination station, journey date, and passenger details, then select an available train and complete the booking.

**FAQ 25: How can I check my train PNR status?**  
Open your train booking in My Trips and use the available PNR status option. You can also check the status through official railway services.

**FAQ 26: What does RAC mean in train booking?**  
RAC stands for Reservation Against Cancellation. It generally permits a passenger to travel, although a full berth may not be available initially.

**FAQ 27: Can I cancel my train ticket on MakeMyTrip?**  
Cancellation may be available through My Trips. Refund eligibility and cancellation charges depend on the ticket status and applicable railway rules.

**FAQ 28: How can I check whether my train ticket is confirmed?**  
Check the booking details and PNR status. The status may indicate Confirmed, RAC, or Waitlisted, depending on the reservation.

**FAQ 29: What happens if my waitlisted train ticket does not get confirmed?**  
The outcome depends on the ticket type and applicable railway rules. Check the latest PNR status and cancellation or refund conditions before travelling.

**FAQ 30: How can I book a bus ticket on MakeMyTrip?**

**FAQ 31: Can I cancel my bus ticket after booking?**  
Cancellation depends on the bus operator's policy and the ticket conditions. Check the cancellation terms before cancelling.

**FAQ 32: How can I find my bus boarding point?**  
Open your booking confirmation or My Trips to view the boarding point, departure time, and operator details.

**FAQ 33: What should I do if my bus is delayed?**  
Check your booking notifications and contact the bus operator or MakeMyTrip support for updated departure information.

**FAQ 34: Can I change my bus boarding point after booking?**  
Changes depend on the operator's rules and available options. Contact the operator or MakeMyTrip support to check whether a modification is possible.
