# Frequently Asked Questions — Booking Checklist Confirm destination, dates, traveller names as per passport, r

### Q: Are flights included in the package cost?
**A:** Packages can be booked with or without flights. Flight-inclusive quotes depend on departure city, dates and airline. The inclusions are shown on each package page.
*(Category: Flights)*

### Q: What is your cancellation and refund policy?
**A:** Cancellation charges depend on the package, the hotel/airline/supplier terms and how close to departure you cancel. The schedule is shown on the package page and booking confirmation. Some packages are non-refundable or carry high deductions, so please read it before paying.
*(Category: Refunds)*

### Q: Do you assist with tourist visas?
**A:** Visa assistance is available for select destinations. Our team shares the document checklist and guidance. Visa approval is always at the embassy/consulate's discretion and is never guaranteed.
*(Category: Visa)*

### Q: Can packages be customized for families?
**A:** Yes. Hotel category, rooming, extra nights, meals and activities can be tailored through a holiday expert before booking confirmation.
*(Category: Customization)*

### Q: What documents do travellers need?
**A:** Valid passport (6+ months validity for international trips), tickets, hotel vouchers, travel insurance and destination-specific forms. Domestic trips need a government photo ID.
*(Category: Documents)*

### Q: Is travel insurance mandatory?
**A:** Strongly recommended, especially for international trips. It can cover medical emergencies, baggage loss and trip interruption. Insurance premiums are generally non-refundable.
*(Category: Insurance)*

### Q: How do I pay for a booking?
**A:** Pay online via UPI, cards or net banking. A booking amount confirms your package; the balance follows the payment schedule on your booking. EMI / pay-later options may be available on select cards and bookings.
*(Category: Payments)*

### Q: What if a flight is delayed or cancelled?
**A:** Contact support with your booking ID. Airline compensation follows airline policy; package land arrangements are adjusted where possible as per package terms.
*(Category: Disruptions)*

### Q: Do prices include tourist taxes and resort fees?
**A:** Inclusions are listed per package. City taxes, resort fees and personal expenses are usually extra unless marked inclusive.
*(Category: Pricing)*

### Q: Can you arrange honeymoon or group departures?
**A:** Yes. Customized honeymoon and group packages are available on request through a holiday expert.
*(Category: Customization)*

### Q: How early should I book peak-season travel?
**A:** For Dec-Jan, summer and festival periods, book 6-10 weeks ahead for better fares and hotel availability.
*(Category: Planning)*

### Q: Are meals included every day?
**A:** Meal plans vary by package (breakfast only, half board, full board). The daily inclusions are listed in the itinerary; declare special diets at booking.
*(Category: Meals)*

### Q: How do I get help during my trip?
**A:** Use the MakeMyTrip app/website support or call the helpline in your booking confirmation. Keep your booking ID handy.
*(Category: Support)*

### Q: What is the child / infant policy?
**A:** Child rates depend on age and bed sharing. Infant policy differs by package and airline; the exact rule is shown on the package page.
*(Category: Family)*
