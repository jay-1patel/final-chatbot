# Claims, Branch Locator & Service Requests

- **Insurance Claims**: Use Insurance & Claims checklist. SBI Life claims line: 1800 267 9090.
- **Branch/ATM Locator**: Provide city or PIN to get IFSC, timings (Mon‑Fri 10:00‑16:00, closed Sun & 2nd/4th Sat).
- **Service Requests**: Statements, nomination, mobile update require authenticated channels or branch visit.
- **Grievance**: Register via `customercare@sbi.co.in` or SMS `UNHAPPY` to 8008202020.
