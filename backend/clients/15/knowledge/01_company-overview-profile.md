# Company Overview

Meditab Software, Inc. develops healthcare technology for electronic health records (EHR), electronic medical records (EMR), medical practice management, pharmacy management, and office workflows. Founded in 1998, Meditab offers solutions for over 40 medical specialties with configurable software architecture, cloud and mobile capabilities, and dedicated implementation support.

## Mission & Vision

Meditab's goal is to develop intuitive technology that helps healthcare providers focus on patients and practice operations. The company emphasizes being a trusted healthcare technology partner to support better outcomes.

## Target Customers

Independent medical practices, multispecialty clinics, physician groups, outpatient facilities, allergy and immunology practices, fertility clinics, ophthalmology practices, cosmetic and aesthetic practices, retail and long-term-care pharmacies, and compounding pharmacies.
