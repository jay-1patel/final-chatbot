# Company Overview

Meditab Software, Inc. develops healthcare technology for electronic health records (EHR), electronic medical records (EMR), medical practice management, pharmacy management, and office workflows. Founded in 1998, the company serves over 40 medical specialties with configurable software architecture and dedicated implementation support.

## Target Customers

Independent medical practices, multispecialty clinics, physician groups, outpatient facilities, allergy and immunology practices, fertility clinics, ophthalmology practices, cosmetic practices, and pharmacies.
