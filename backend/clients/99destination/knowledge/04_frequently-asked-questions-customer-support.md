# Frequently Asked Questions — 99 Destinations

### Q: Are flights included in the package cost?
**A:** Packages are offered with or without flights. Flight-inclusive quotes depend on departure city, dates, and airline class.
*(Category: Flights)*

### Q: What is your cancellation and refund policy?
**A:** Cancel 21+ days before departure: typically 90% refund of land package. 7·20 days: partial refund per supplier rules. Inside 7 days: largely non-refundable unless travel insurance covers it.
*(Category: Refunds)*

### Q: Do you assist with tourist visas?
**A:** Yes. Our visa desk reviews documents, books appointments where needed, and tracks application status. Visa approval is always at the consulate·s discretion.
*(Category: Visa)*

### Q: Can packages be customized for families?
**A:** Yes · rooming, kid meals, accessibility needs, extra nights, and activity swaps can be tailored before booking confirmation.
*(Category: Customization)*

### Q: What documents do travellers need?
**A:** Valid passport (6+ months validity for international), tickets, hotel vouchers, travel insurance, and destination-specific forms. Domestic trips need government photo ID.
*(Category: Documents)*

### Q: Is travel insurance mandatory?
**A:** Strongly recommended for international trips; some packages bundle basic cover. Medical evacuation and trip cancellation add-ons are available.
*(Category: Insurance)*

### Q: How do I pay for a booking?
**A:** Booking amount secures seats; balance is due as per invoice schedule. We accept UPI, cards, net banking, and bank transfer. EMI options on select cards.
*(Category: Payments)*

### Q: What if a flight is delayed or cancelled?
**A:** We coordinate rebooking with airlines/hotels. Compensation follows airline policy; land arrangements are adjusted where possible.
*(Category: Disruptions)*

### Q: Do prices include tourist taxes and resort fees?
**A:** Inclusions are listed per package. City taxes, resort fees, and personal expenses are usually extra unless marked inclusive.
*(Category: Pricing)*

### Q: Can you arrange honeymoon or group departures?
**A:** Yes · honeymoon upgrades, private transfers, and group blocks with tour managers are available on request.
*(Category: Customization)*

### Q: How early should I book peak-season travel?
**A:** For Dec·Jan and school holidays, book 6·10 weeks ahead for better airfares and hotel inventory.
*(Category: Planning)*

### Q: Are meals included every day?
**A:** Meal plans vary (BB, MAP, AP). Daily inclusions are listed in the itinerary; special diets should be declared at booking.
*(Category: Meals)*

### Q: Do you provide 24x7 emergency support on trip?
**A:** Yes. Guests receive a destination duty phone for medical, lost passport, or logistics emergencies.
*(Category: Support)*

### Q: What is the child / infant policy?
**A:** Child rates depend on age and bed sharing. Infants usually travel free on land packages excluding flights and entrance fees.
*(Category: Family)*
