# Send2Digital Platform Overview

Send2Digital (send2.digital) is Leeway Softech's associated omnichannel CPaaS platform for conversation marketing. Offers SMS, email, WhatsApp, RCS, push notifications, chatbot, verified SMS, and missed-call services.
