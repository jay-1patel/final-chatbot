# CPaaS: Communications Platform as a Service

CPaaS is a cloud-based communications layer exposing channels through web interfaces, APIs, or integrations. Connects messaging to CRM, e-commerce, service desk, or internal workflows.

Common channels: A2P/bulk SMS, email, WhatsApp, RCS, push notifications, chatbot, verified SMS, missed-call services, voice/IVR, SMS Gateway.

Use cases: Order/appointment updates, reminders, service alerts, two-way support conversations, promotional campaigns. OTP/authentication supported when account/channel are configured.

Limits: Delivery, throughput, sender IDs, template approvals, and charges vary by country/channel. Never guarantee universal coverage or delivery.
