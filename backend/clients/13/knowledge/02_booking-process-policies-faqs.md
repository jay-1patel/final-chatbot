# Booking Process, Policies & FAQs\n\n## Home Buying Journey & Booking Process\n1. **Discover** – Browse the catalog and shortlist projects by location, configuration and budget.\n2. **Site Visit** – Schedule a guided tour; receive cost sheets and brochures.\n3. **Booking** – Pay the booking amount, complete KYC and lock the unit.\n4. **Agreement** – Sign the builder‑buyer agreement; registration as per state rules.\n5. **Milestones** – Follow the payment plan and retain receipts.\n6. **Possession** – Snagging, OC verification, handover and society onboarding.\n\n## Frequently Asked Questions\n### What banks have approved home loans for these projects?\nMost major banks and housing finance companies finance Prestige projects, subject to eligibility. The sales team provides the approved‑bank list and sanction details.\n\n### Is the site visit pickup complimentary?\nCab pickup is arranged on request when you schedule a site visit.\n\n### What is the booking advance amount?\nAdvance varies per project and unit; refer to the official cost sheet shared by sales. All‑inclusive prices include GST and possession charges.\n\n### Are all projects RERA certified?\nAll listed projects display their RERA registration numbers. Older completed projects may pre‑date RERA.\n\n### Can NRIs purchase units?\nYes, NRIs can buy through NRE/NRO channels. Our Dubai office assists overseas buyers.\n\n### What is included in the possession handover kit?\nKeys, Occupancy Certificate (OC) details and possession formalities.\n\n### Do you offer construction‑linked payment plans?\nStandard payment plans are in the cost sheet; custom plans need sales‑head approval.\n\n### How are stamp duty and registration handled?\nBuyer pays stamp duty and registration as per Karnataka rates; our documentation team coordinates the process.\n\n### How do I raise a post‑sales complaint?\nMessage your booking ID with the issue or use **Customer Support** in the menu. You can also call 1800 313 0080 or email properties@prestigeconstructions.com.
