# Company Overview

Leeway Softech Pvt Ltd is an India-based technology company founded in **2012**, headquartered in **Mumbai**. We provide AI development, custom software development, enterprise IT solutions, CPaaS, data and analytics, and digital transformation services. We serve government bodies, financial institutions, enterprises, education, hospitality, politics and other sectors.

**Tagline:** *Innovative Technology. Creative Work. Trusted Delivery.*

**Impact numbers:**
- 1500+ clients served
- 14 years in operation
- 120+ projects delivered (Communication & CPaaS)
- 200+ campaigns run (Digital Growth & Marketing)
- 60+ apps launched (Web & Mobile)
- 45+ AI solutions built
- 0 breaches recorded (Enterprise Hosting & Security)

**Certifications:**
- ISO 9001:2015
- ISO/IEC 27001:2013
- CMMI Level 3
