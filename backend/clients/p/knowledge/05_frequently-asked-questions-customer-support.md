# Frequently Asked Questions — Prestige Group

### Q: What banks have approved home loans for these projects?
**A:** Most major banks and housing finance companies offer home loans for Prestige projects, subject to eligibility. The sales team shares the approved-bank list and sanction details for your chosen project. [Confirm bank list with client]
*(Category: Home Loans)*

### Q: Is the site visit pickup complimentary?
**A:** Site visits can be scheduled through this menu. Cab pickup availability is confirmed by the sales team at booking. [Confirm policy with client]
*(Category: Site Visits)*

### Q: What is the booking advance amount?
**A:** The booking amount differs by project and unit. It is listed in the official cost sheet shared by the sales team; the balance follows the project payment plan. Quoted all-inclusive prices cover base cost, applicable GST and possession charges. [Confirm amounts with client]
*(Category: Booking & Payments)*

### Q: Are all projects RERA certified?
**A:** Prestige projects display their RERA registration numbers, listed against each property in the catalog. You can verify any ID on the Karnataka RERA portal (rera.karnataka.gov.in). Older completed projects may pre-date RERA.
*(Category: Legal & Approvals)*

### Q: Can Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) purchase units?
**A:** Yes. NRIs can purchase residential and commercial property in India through NRE/NRO channels, subject to RBI/FEMA rules. Prestige also has an international office in Dubai to assist overseas buyers. [Confirm NRI desk details]
*(Category: NRI Purchase)*

### Q: What is included in the possession handover kit?
**A:** Handover typically includes keys, occupancy certificate (OC) details, and possession formalities. The OC is applied for closer to the possession date as per regulatory timelines. [Confirm kit contents with client]
*(Category: Possession)*

### Q: Do you offer construction-linked payment plans?
**A:** Payment plans for each project are published in the official cost sheet. Custom plans need sales-head approval. [Confirm with client, including which projects are construction-linked]
*(Category: Booking & Payments)*

### Q: Can I upgrade my unit configuration later?
**A:** Unit upgrades depend on available inventory and stage of construction. Differential pricing and an agreement addendum may apply.
*(Category: Inventory)*

### Q: What amenities are common across residential projects?
**A:** Typical amenities include clubhouse, swimming pool, gym, landscaped gardens, security, power backup and parking. Exact lists vary by project.
*(Category: Amenities)*

### Q: How are stamp duty and registration handled?
**A:** Stamp duty and registration are paid by the buyer as per Karnataka state rates. The documentation team coordinates the appointment and checklist.
*(Category: Legal & Approvals)*

### Q: What is your resale / transfer policy before possession?
**A:** Pre-possession transfers need builder NOC, a transfer fee as per the agreement, and KYC of the incoming buyer. [Confirm with client]
*(Category: Transfers)*

### Q: How do I raise a post-sales complaint?
**A:** Message your booking ID with the issue, or use Customer Support in the menu. You can also call 1800 313 0080 or email properties@prestigeconstructions.com.
*(Category: Support)*

### Q: Are floor plans and carpet areas approximate?
**A:** Marketing floor plans are indicative. Carpet area and final layout follow the RERA-approved drawings attached to your agreement.
*(Category: Floor Plans)*

### Q: Do commercial leases include CAM charges?
**A:** Lease quotes typically exclude CAM, property tax and parking unless stated. Ask for the commercial term sheet for the full break-up. [Confirm with client]
*(Category: Commercial)*
