# Home Buying Journey 1. **Discover** · Browse catalog properties and shortlist by location, configuration, and budget. 2. **Site visit** · Schedule a complimentary cab visit; collect cost sheet and brochure. 3. **Booking** · Pay booking advance, complete KYC, and lock the unit. 4. **Agreement** · Sign builder-buyer agreement; registration as per state rules. 5. **Milestones** · Follow construction-linked payments; keep receipts. 6. **Possession** · Snagging, OC verification, handover kit, and society onboarding. Always rely on RERA IDs and sanctioned plans over marketing creatives.
