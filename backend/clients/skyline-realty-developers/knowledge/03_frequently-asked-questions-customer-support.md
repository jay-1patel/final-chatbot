# Frequently Asked Questions — Skyline Realty & Developers

### Q: What banks have approved home loans for these projects?
**A:** Approved by SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, and Kotak with pre-approved sanction letters and financing up to 80·90% of the agreement value.
*(Category: Home Loans)*

### Q: Is the site visit pickup complimentary?
**A:** Yes. We arrange a dedicated AC cab pickup and drop from your residence or office at no charge, typically within the same city limits.
*(Category: Site Visits)*

### Q: What is the booking advance amount?
**A:** A booking advance of ·2,00,000 secures your selected unit. The balance follows the construction-linked payment plan in the cost sheet.
*(Category: Booking & Payments)*

### Q: Are all projects RERA certified?
**A:** Yes. Every live inventory project holds valid RERA registration and municipal sanctions. RERA IDs are listed against each property in the catalog.
*(Category: Legal & Approvals)*

### Q: Can Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) purchase units?
**A:** Yes. NRIs can buy residential or commercial units via NRE/NRO channels with full RBI compliance. Our NRI desk assists with power of attorney and remittance paperwork.
*(Category: NRI Purchase)*

### Q: What is included in the possession handover kit?
**A:** Handover includes keys, occupancy certificate copy, society bye-laws summary, snag checklist sign-off, and introduction to facility management.
*(Category: Possession)*

### Q: Do you offer construction-linked payment plans?
**A:** Yes. Milestone plans are published in the official cost sheet (booking, foundation, slab stages, finishing, possession). Custom plans need sales-head approval.
*(Category: Booking & Payments)*

### Q: Can I upgrade my unit configuration later?
**A:** Unit upgrades depend on available inventory and stage of construction. Differential pricing and a fresh agreement addendum apply.
*(Category: Inventory)*

### Q: What amenities are common across residential projects?
**A:** Typical amenities include clubhouse, swimming pool, gym, landscaped gardens, 24x7 security, power backup, and visitor parking. Exact lists vary by project.
*(Category: Amenities)*

### Q: How are stamp duty and registration handled?
**A:** Stamp duty and registration are paid by the buyer as per state rates. Our documentation team coordinates the appointment and checklist.
*(Category: Legal & Approvals)*

### Q: What is your resale / transfer policy before possession?
**A:** Pre-possession transfers require builder NOC, transfer fee as per agreement, and updated KYC of the incoming buyer.
*(Category: Transfers)*

### Q: How do I raise a post-sales complaint?
**A:** Message your booking ID with the issue, or use Customer Support in the menu. Tickets are acknowledged within 1 business day.
*(Category: Support)*

### Q: Are floor plans and carpet areas approximate?
**A:** Marketing floor plans are indicative. Carpet area and final layout follow the RERA-approved drawings attached to your agreement.
*(Category: Floor Plans)*

### Q: Do commercial leases include CAM charges?
**A:** Lease quotes typically exclude CAM, property tax, and parking unless stated. Ask for the commercial term sheet for full break-up.
*(Category: Commercial)*
