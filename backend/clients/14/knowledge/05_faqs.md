# Frequently Asked Questions

## Company Information
- What is ZURU Toys? ZURU Toys is a global toy company that develops and distributes toys and play products.
- What are ZURU's popular toy brands? XSHOT, Mini Brands, Rainbocorns, Pets Alive, Robo Alive, Bunch O Balloons, Fuggler, Smashers, 5 Surprise, and Snackles.
- What is ZURU Tech? ZURU Tech is the technology arm of ZURU. Website: www.zuru.tech. Careers: careers.tech@zuru.com.

## Purchasing Information
- Where can I buy ZURU toys? Through major retailers and channels listed on https://zurutoys.com/. Availability varies by country.
- How can I find a toy's price? Check the retailer listing for the exact product and country. Prices vary by region and store.
- Are all ZURU toys available in every country? No. Availability depends on country, retailer, distribution, and stock.

## Safety and Usage
- How do I find the correct age recommendation? Check the packaging and instructions for that specific product. Do not invent age ratings.
- Can I use rechargeable batteries? Only when the product instructions allow it. Match the required battery type and voltage.

## Returns and Support
- How do I report a damaged toy? Keep proof of purchase, photograph the damage, and contact the retailer or official support.
- What should I do if a toy is missing a piece? Contact support with product name, item number, purchase details, and missing part description.
- How can I contact ZURU in India? Ahmedabad: +91 81288 64506, india@zuru.com. Kolkata: +91 90383 84033.
- What information should I give customer support? Product name and brand, item/model number, purchase date/place, issue description, photos, and proof of purchase.
