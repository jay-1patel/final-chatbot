# Company Overview

ZURU Toys is a global toy company focused on creativity, innovation, imaginative play, and accessible products for children and families. The company uses automation, manufacturing capabilities, and product innovation to develop toys for international markets. ZURU distributes its products through major retailers in more than 120 countries.

**Mission and Values**:
- Encourage imagination and creative play
- Develop engaging and innovative toys
- Improve product quality and affordability
- Use automation and manufacturing innovation
- Support responsible business practices and sustainability
