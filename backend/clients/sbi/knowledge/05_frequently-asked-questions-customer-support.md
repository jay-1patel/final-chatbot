# Frequently Asked Questions — Stay Safe

### Q: How do I immediately block a lost or stolen card?
**A:** Type Block Card or tap Block Lost Card and confirm the last 4 digits. You can also SMS BLOCK to 567676 from your registered mobile, or call 1800 1234 / 1800 2100.
*(Category: Card Security)*

### Q: Can financial transactions be made through WhatsApp?
**A:** No. This assistant helps with information, eligibility, and service requests only. Never share OTPs, PINs, or passwords here.
*(Category: Security Guardrail)*

### Q: What documents are required to open a savings account?
**A:** Aadhaar, PAN (or Form 60) and a photograph. Eligible customers can start the process online through YONO SBI and complete KYC.
*(Category: Account Opening)*

### Q: How do I submit an insurance claim?
**A:** Use Insurance & Claims for the checklist. For SBI Life or SBI General, keep your policy number and documents ready and contact the respective insurer's claim helpline.
*(Category: Claims)*

### Q: How can I find my IFSC and branch details?
**A:** Share your city or PIN, or use Locate Branch & ATM. SBI IFSC codes start with SBIN0 and are printed on cheque books and passbooks.
*(Category: Branch Info)*

### Q: Is there a minimum balance for a savings account?
**A:** SBI has waived the Average Monthly Balance requirement for regular savings accounts. Other charges apply as per the schedule of charges.
*(Category: Accounts)*

### Q: How do I activate net banking or mobile banking?
**A:** Use the official SBI net banking portal or YONO SBI app. Activation is through authenticated channels. The chatbot will never ask for your password.
*(Category: Digital Banking)*

### Q: What should I do if I see an unauthorized transaction?
**A:** Block the card immediately, then report it to the bank and raise a dispute ticket with date, amount, and merchant. You can also report cyber fraud on 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in.
*(Category: Fraud)*

### Q: Are senior citizens eligible for higher FD rates?
**A:** Yes. Senior citizens receive an additional interest rate on eligible term deposits (about 0.50% p.a.). Check current rates on bank.sbi.
*(Category: Deposits)*

### Q: How long does home loan sanction take?
**A:** Timelines depend on documents, property verification and credit assessment. Ask for the latest document checklist before applying.
*(Category: Loans)*

### Q: Can I update my registered mobile number?
**A:** Visit your home branch with KYC or use authenticated channels such as SBI ATM or net banking where available. WhatsApp cannot change registered numbers alone.
*(Category: Profile)*

### Q: What are branch working hours?
**A:** Typically 10:00 AM - 04:00 PM on weekdays and 1st/3rd/5th Saturdays; 2nd/4th Saturdays and Sundays are closed. Confirm for your branch as timings can vary.
*(Category: Branch Info)*

### Q: Do you offer nomination facilities?
**A:** Yes. Nomination can be registered for savings, deposits, and lockers as per bank policy.
*(Category: Accounts)*

### Q: How do I download account statements?
**A:** Use official net banking or the YONO SBI app for PDF statements. The assistant can explain steps but cannot send statements without authenticated channels.
*(Category: Statements)*
