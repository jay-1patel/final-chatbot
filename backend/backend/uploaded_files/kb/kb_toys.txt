1. Company Overview
Company Name: ZURU Toys
Parent Organization: ZURU Group
Industry: Toy design, development, manufacturing, and distribution
Official Website: https://zurutoys.com/
Brand Tagline: Play Reimagined.

Company Description
ZURU Toys is a global toy company focused on creativity, innovation, imaginative play, and accessible products for children and families. The company uses automation, manufacturing capabilities, and product innovation to develop toys for international markets.

ZURU distributes its products through major retailers in more than 120 countries, according to its official website.

Use automation and manufacturing innovation.

5. Product Safety
Customers should follow the age recommendations, warnings, and instructions supplied with each product.

Safety guidance:

Keep small parts away from children who may swallow them.
Use electronic toys according to their battery instructions.
Do not use damaged toys or damaged battery compartments.
Use water toys and outdoor products according to their instructions.
Stop using a product if a safety concern arises.
Contact the retailer or manufacturer to report potential product defects.
The chatbot must not provide an age rating or safety certification unless it has been verified for the exact product.

6. Troubleshooting
Toy Does Not Work
Check the product instructions and, for electronic toys, verify that the batteries are correctly installed and suitable for the model.

Ask the customer for the product name, item number, purchase details, and a description of the missing component. Refer the customer to official support.

Damaged Product
Recommend keeping the packaging and purchase receipt and taking photographs of the damage before contacting the retailer or manufacturer.

Cleaning

7. Toy use, safety, and troubleshooting
Q36. How do I know the recommended age for a toy?

Check the age recommendation and safety warnings printed on the specific product's packaging or instructions.

Q37. Are ZURU toys safe for young children?

Use each toy according to its stated age recommendation and warnings. Keep products containing small parts away from children who may put them in their mouths.

Q38. How do I assemble a ZURU toy?

Follow the instructions supplied with the product. If the instructions are missing, contact customer support and provide the product name and item number.

Q39. What should I do if my toy is not working?

Check the instructions, battery installation if applicable, and any product-specific troubleshooting guidance. If the issue persists, contact ZURU support.

Q40. What should I do if an electronic toy does not turn on?

Check that the batteries are the correct type and are installed in the correct direction. Follow the product instructions and avoid using a damaged battery compartment.

Q41. Can I wash or clean a ZURU toy?

Cleaning requirements depend on the product. Follow the manufacturer's instructions and avoid immersing electronic toys in water unless the product is specifically designed for it.

Q42. Can I use rechargeable batteries in ZURU toys?

Only use rechargeable batteries when permitted by the specific product's instructions. Check the recommended battery type and voltage first.

Q43. What should I do if a toy arrives damaged?

Keep the packaging and purchase receipt, take photographs of the damage, and contact the retailer or ZURU's customer service team.

Q44. What if a toy is missing a piece?

Contact customer support with the product name, item number, purchase details, and a description of the missing component. Replacement availability will depend on the product and applicable policy.

Q45. Can I get replacement parts for ZURU toys?

Contact ZURU customer support to ask whether replacement parts are available for your particular model. Availability is not guaranteed for every product.

Q46. What should I do if a water toy leaks?

Check the product instructions for correct assembly and use. Stop using it if it appears damaged, and contact support if the problem continues.

Q47. What should I do if a toy has a strong or unusual smell?

Stop using the toy if the smell is concerning, keep it away from young children, and contact the seller or manufacturer for guidance.

Q48. Can I repair a broken ZURU toy myself?

Follow only manufacturer-approved repair guidance. Avoid modifying electronic components, blasters, pressurized mechanisms, or battery compartments.
