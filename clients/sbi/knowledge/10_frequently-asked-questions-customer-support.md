# Frequently Asked Questions — Digital Banking Help

### Q: How do I activate net banking for the first time?
**A:** Use New User Registration on the official SBI net banking portal or visit your home branch. Activation is through authenticated channels. The chatbot will never ask for your password.
*(Category: Digital Banking)*

### Q: I forgot my net banking password. What should I do?
**A:** Use the Forgot Password option on the official portal with your registered mobile, or visit your home branch. Never share OTPs or passwords in this chat.
*(Category: Digital Banking)*

### Q: How do I register for the YONO SBI app?
**A:** Install YONO SBI only from the official app store, register with the mobile number linked to your account and set your own MPIN. SBI never asks for it over chat.
*(Category: Digital Banking)*

### Q: My YONO SBI app is not opening. What can I do?
**A:** Update the app from the official store, restart your phone and check your internet connection. If it still fails, raise a ticket with your phone model and the error shown.
*(Category: Digital Banking)*

### Q: Is it safe to use YONO SBI on public Wi-Fi?
**A:** Avoid banking on public Wi-Fi where possible. Use your own mobile data and keep your phone locked with a screen lock.
*(Category: Digital Banking)*

### Q: How do I set up UPI with SBI?
**A:** Link your account and set a UPI PIN inside an official UPI app such as YONO SBI. Never share your UPI PIN with anyone, including this assistant.
*(Category: UPI)*

### Q: Can I make a UPI payment through this WhatsApp chat?
**A:** No. This assistant does not move money. Use your official UPI app for payments.
*(Category: UPI)*

### Q: My UPI payment failed but money was debited. What now?
**A:** Failed UPI debits are usually reversed automatically within a few working days. If not, raise a ticket with the transaction ID, date and amount.
*(Category: UPI)*

### Q: What are the UPI transaction limits?
**A:** Limits are set by NPCI and the bank, and can differ by transaction type. Check the limits shown inside your UPI app or on bank.sbi.
*(Category: UPI)*

### Q: Can I see my balance or mini statement here?
**A:** No. This assistant cannot share balances or statements without authenticated channels. Use YONO SBI, net banking, an ATM or the registered-mobile services listed on bank.sbi.
*(Category: Statements)*

### Q: How do I update my passbook?
**A:** Visit your home branch or a self-service passbook printer with your passbook and update it. Branch staff can help if the kiosk is unavailable.
*(Category: Statements)*

### Q: How do I get an interest or account certificate?
**A:** Request it through net banking, YONO SBI or at your home branch. The assistant can explain the steps but cannot issue certificates here.
*(Category: Statements)*

### Q: Who can open a PPF account and what are the deposit limits?
**A:** Any resident individual can open one PPF account. Minimum ₹500 and maximum ₹1.5 lakh per financial year. The rate is notified by the Government, so check bank.sbi for the current rate.
*(Category: Govt Schemes)*

### Q: Can I take a loan or withdraw from my PPF account?
**A:** Loan and partial withdrawal facilities are allowed only after the years set by scheme rules. Check the scheme conditions or ask your branch before applying.
*(Category: Govt Schemes)*

### Q: Who can open a Sukanya Samriddhi account?
**A:** A parent or guardian can open it for a girl child under 10 years of age. Minimum ₹250 and maximum ₹1.5 lakh per year. Carry the child's birth certificate and guardian KYC.
*(Category: Govt Schemes)*

### Q: When does a Sukanya Samriddhi account mature?
**A:** The account matures 21 years after it is opened. Deposits are made for the first 15 years. Partial withdrawal for education is allowed as per scheme rules.
*(Category: Govt Schemes)*

### Q: What is Atal Pension Yojana?
**A:** A Government pension scheme for eligible citizens aged 18-40 with a savings account. A fixed monthly pension is payable from age 60 based on the contribution plan chosen.
*(Category: Govt Schemes)*

### Q: Can income-tax payers join Atal Pension Yojana?
**A:** As per current scheme rules, income-tax payers are not eligible to join. Confirm the latest eligibility with your branch before enrolling.
*(Category: Govt Schemes)*

### Q: Who can open a Senior Citizens Savings Scheme account?
**A:** Citizens aged 60 or above, and certain early retirees under scheme conditions. The tenure is 5 years and can be extended. Interest rates are notified by the Government.
*(Category: Pension & Seniors)*

### Q: How do pensioners submit a life certificate?
**A:** Pensioners submit it every year through their pension-paying branch, or digitally through the official Jeevan Pramaan facility. Check with the branch for the due month.
*(Category: Pension & Seniors)*

### Q: Is doorstep banking available for senior citizens?
**A:** Doorstep banking is offered to eligible senior citizens and differently abled customers as per bank criteria. Ask your home branch about availability and charges.
*(Category: Pension & Seniors)*

### Q: My pension was not credited. Where do I check?
**A:** Contact the pension-paying branch with your PPO number. If it remains unresolved, raise a complaint through Raise a Complaint with the details.
*(Category: Pension & Seniors)*

### Q: Can NRIs open an account with SBI?
**A:** Yes. NRE, NRO and FCNR(B) accounts are available. Keep your passport, visa / OCI and overseas address proof ready, and confirm the process with the branch or the NRI helpdesk.
*(Category: NRI Banking)*

### Q: What is the difference between NRE and NRO accounts?
**A:** NRE holds foreign earnings in rupees and is repatriable. NRO is for income earned in India and repatriation is subject to RBI rules. Ask the branch which one fits you.
*(Category: NRI Banking)*

### Q: How can I receive money from abroad?
**A:** Share your account number, branch IFSC and the bank's SWIFT code with the sender for an inward remittance. This assistant does not move or track money.
*(Category: NRI Banking)*

### Q: Can I operate my NRI account from abroad?
**A:** Yes, through official net banking and YONO SBI where enabled. Keep your registered mobile and email updated with the bank.
*(Category: NRI Banking)*

### Q: How do I apply for a safe deposit locker?
**A:** Visit your branch with KYC to check availability and the annual rent for each locker size. Allotment is subject to availability and a signed locker agreement.
*(Category: Lockers)*

### Q: What can I keep in a bank locker?
**A:** Documents, jewellery and valuables permitted by the locker agreement. Cash, illegal items and hazardous goods are not allowed.
*(Category: Lockers)*

### Q: How do I add or change a nominee?
**A:** Nomination can be registered or changed at your branch with the nomination form, or through net banking where available. The assistant can explain the steps only.
*(Category: Nomination)*

### Q: Can I nominate more than one person?
**A:** Yes, subject to bank policy and the account type. The branch will record each nominee's share. Ask for the nomination form at your branch.
*(Category: Nomination)*

### Q: How do I complete my re-KYC?
**A:** Submit updated ID and address proof (such as Aadhaar and PAN) at your home branch, or through net banking / YONO SBI where the option is available.
*(Category: KYC)*

### Q: Can I change my address, mobile or email on WhatsApp?
**A:** No. WhatsApp cannot change registered details. Use your home branch with KYC or an authenticated channel such as net banking or an SBI ATM.
*(Category: Profile)*

### Q: What documents are needed for an education loan?
**A:** Typically admission letter, fee structure, ID and address proof, academic records and co-applicant income proof. Ask your branch for the latest checklist before applying.
*(Category: Education Loan)*

### Q: Do I have to repay while I am studying?
**A:** Education loans usually have a moratorium during the course and for a short period after. Repayment terms depend on the scheme, so confirm with the branch.
*(Category: Education Loan)*

### Q: Can I get an education loan for studying abroad?
**A:** Yes, SBI offers education loans for study abroad. Eligibility, documents and collateral norms depend on the loan amount, so check bank.sbi or visit a branch.
*(Category: Education Loan)*

### Q: How can I take a gold loan?
**A:** Visit your branch with your gold ornaments and KYC. The gold is valued and the loan amount follows bank norms. Check bank.sbi for the current rate.
*(Category: Loans)*

### Q: How do I spot a phishing message?
**A:** Be careful with links, urgent threats and requests for OTP, PIN or CVV. SBI never asks for these on phone, SMS or WhatsApp. Do not click unknown links.
*(Category: Cyber Safety)*

### Q: Where do I report cyber fraud?
**A:** Call 1930 or report at cybercrime.gov.in as soon as possible. Also block your card and inform the bank with the amount, time and merchant.
*(Category: Cyber Safety)*

### Q: Should I install apps shared through links or screen-sharing?
**A:** No. Install apps only from official stores and never allow screen-sharing or remote-access apps for banking, as this is a common fraud method.
*(Category: Cyber Safety)*

### Q: How do I keep my accounts safe?
**A:** Use strong, unique passwords, enable screen lock, check statements often and update your registered mobile. Never share OTP, PIN or CVV with anyone.
*(Category: Cyber Safety)*

### Q: Where can I see the service charges?
**A:** The schedule of charges is published on bank.sbi and available at branches. Charges can change, so verify before relying on any number.
*(Category: Charges)*

### Q: Are there limits on free ATM transactions?
**A:** Free ATM transaction limits depend on the account type and location. Check your account's terms on bank.sbi or ask your branch.
*(Category: Charges)*

### Q: Why was an SMS alert or service fee charged?
**A:** Some services, such as SMS alerts or card services, may carry charges as per the schedule. Check your statement or raise a ticket with the date and amount.
*(Category: Charges)*

### Q: How much should I save every month?
**A:** A common starting point is to keep an emergency fund and set a regular saving goal. Choose a plan that suits your income and talk to a branch advisor for personal advice.
*(Category: Financial Literacy)*

### Q: What are good habits for safe banking?
**A:** Check statements regularly, set transaction alerts, keep nominees updated and never share secret credentials. Use only official SBI channels.
*(Category: Financial Literacy)*
