# Northwind Capital — Services & Engagement

Northwind Capital is a financial advisory firm covering tax planning, business
compliance, and investment advisory for individuals and SMEs.

## Services

### Tax Planning & Filing
- Individual income tax filing (salary, capital gains, F&O)
- Business tax planning and advance tax computation
- ITR preparation, review, and filing support
- Response drafting for income tax notices

### Business Compliance
- GST registration, monthly/quarterly filing, and annual returns
- TDS returns and payroll compliance
- Company incorporation, annual ROC filings, and statutory registers
- Bookkeeping and management accounting on monthly retainers

### Investment Advisory
- Goal-based financial planning (retirement, education, home)
- Mutual fund portfolio review and rebalancing suggestions
- Insurance need analysis (term, health)
- Advisory is delivered only after a signed engagement letter and a documented
  risk profile

## Engagement
- First consultation is free (30 minutes)
- Fixed-fee filing engagements; monthly retainers for compliance work
- Advisory fees are as per the engagement letter — the bot never quotes fees
- A named advisor is assigned to every engagement

## Compliance Calendar (indicative)
- GST GSTR-1: 11th of every month; GSTR-3B: 20th of every month
- TDS payment: 7th of every month; TDS returns: quarterly
- Advance tax: 15 Jun / 15 Sep / 15 Dec / 15 Mar
- ITR: typically 31 July for individuals (subject to CBDT extension)

Deadlines change — the team confirms current due dates during engagement.
