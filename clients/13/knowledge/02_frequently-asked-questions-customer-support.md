Product Safety FAQs
*(Category: General)*

### Q: How do I know the recommended age for a toy?
**A:** Check the age recommendation and safety warnings printed on the specific product's packaging or
instructions.
*(Category: General)*

### Q: Are ZURU toys safe for young children?
**A:** Use each toy according to its stated age recommendation and warnings. Keep products containing small
parts away from children who may put them in their mouths.
*(Category: General)*

### Q: How do I assemble a ZURU toy?
**A:** Follow the instructions supplied with the product. If the instructions are missing, contact customer support
and provide the product name and item number.
*(Category: General)*

### Q: What should I do if my toy is not working?
**A:** Check the instructions, battery installation if applicable, and any product-specific troubleshooting guidance.
If the issue persists, contact ZURU support.
*(Category: General)*

### Q: What should I do if an electronic toy does not turn on?
**A:** Check that the batteries are the correct type and are installed in the correct direction. Follow the product
instructions and avoid using a damaged battery compartment.

Returns and Warranty FAQs
*(Category: General)*

### Q: What should I do if a toy is missing a piece?
**A:** Contact customer support with the product name, item number, purchase details, and a description of the
missing component. Replacement availability will depend on the product and applicable policy.
*(Category: General)*

### Q: Can I get replacement parts for ZURU toys?
**A:** Contact ZURU customer support to ask whether replacement parts are available for your particular model.
Availability is not guaranteed for every product.
*(Category: General)*

### Q: What should I do if a water toy leaks?
**A:** Check the product instructions for correct assembly and use. Stop using it if it appears damaged, and
contact support if the problem continues.
*(Category: General)*

### Q: What should I do if a toy has a strong or unusual smell?
**A:** Stop using the toy if the smell is concerning, keep it away from young children, and contact the seller or
manufacturer for guidance.
*(Category: General)*

### Q: Can I repair a broken ZURU toy myself?
**A:** Follow only manufacturer-approved repair guidance. Avoid modifying electronic components, blasters,
pressurized mechanisms, or battery compartments.
*(Category: General)*

### Q: Does ZURU provide a warranty for its toys?
**A:** Warranty coverage depends on the product, country, and applicable terms. Check the product
documentation or contact customer support for confirmation.
*(Category: General)*

### Q: Can I return a ZURU toy if I am not satisfied?
**A:** Returns are generally handled according to the retailer's return policy and applicable local consumer laws.
Contact the store where you purchased the toy.
Customer Support FAQs
*(Category: General)*

### Q: What information should I provide when contacting customer support?
**A:** Provide the following details where applicable: product name and brand, item or model number, date and
place of purchase, description of the issue, photographs of the product or defect, and proof of purchase, if
available.
*(Category: General)*

### Q: How long does ZURU take to respond to customer enquiries?
**A:** Response times can vary. ZURU's published privacy policy says its customer service team aims to
respond to legitimate requests within one week, although complex cases may take longer.
*(Category: General)*

### Q: Can I track an order placed with a retailer?
**A:** If you purchased through a third-party retailer, use that retailer's order-tracking system or contact its
customer service team.
