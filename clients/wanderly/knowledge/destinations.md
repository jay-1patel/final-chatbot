# Wanderly Travel — Destinations

Wanderly Travel crafts guided and custom tour packages across domestic and
international destinations.

## Domestic (India)
- Kerala backwaters — houseboats, Munnar tea gardens, Alleppey (5N/6D)
- Rajasthan — Jaipur, Udaipur, Jaisalmer desert camps (6N/7D)
- Himachal — Manali, Spiti valley circuits (5N/6D)
- Goa — beaches, heritage walks, weekend getaways (3N/4D)
- Andaman — Port Blair, Havelock scuba and beaches (4N/5D)

## International
- Bali, Indonesia — beaches, temples, Ubud (5N/6D)
- Dubai, UAE — city tours, desert safari (4N/5D)
- Thailand — Bangkok, Pattaya (5N/6D)
- Singapore + Malaysia combo (6N/7D)
- Europe — Paris, Switzerland, Italy grand tour (10N/11D)

Every package includes accommodation, daily breakfast, sightseeing as listed
in the itinerary, and an on-trip coordinator. Flights and visa fees are
typically excluded unless the itinerary says otherwise.
