# Wanderly Travel — Packages, Booking Policy & Visa

## What's included in a package
- Accommodation in listed hotels or houseboats
- Daily breakfast (half-board available on request)
- All transfers and sightseeing per the itinerary
- On-trip coordinator and 24x7 emergency helpline during the trip

## What's usually excluded
- Flights, visa fees, and travel insurance unless stated
- Lunches and dinners unless the itinerary lists them
- Personal expenses, tips, and adventure activities

## Booking Policy
- Bookings are confirmed on 30% advance payment
- Full payment is due 15 days before departure
- Free date change up to 21 days before departure (subject to availability)
- Cancellation 30+ days before departure: 90% refund
- Cancellation 15-29 days before departure: 50% refund
- Cancellations within 14 days of departure: no refund
- Group bookings (10+ travellers) get a dedicated coordinator and custom itinerary

## Visa & Documents
- Wanderly assists with visa applications for Dubai, Thailand, Bali, and Europe
- You provide: passport (6+ months validity), photos, bank statements, and
  employment or business proof
- Processing time: typically 5-15 working days depending on destination
- Wanderly assists with paperwork but never guarantees visa approval
- For domestic packages: carry a government photo ID; no visa needed

## Support
- Business hours: Mon-Sat, 9:00-19:00 IST
- Emergency on-trip helpline runs 24x7 during your travel dates
- Raise a ticket on WhatsApp anytime; the team responds in business hours
