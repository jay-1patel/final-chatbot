# Frequently Asked Questions — Stay Safe Block lost cards immediately via the menu, SMS BLOCK to 567676 or 1800

### Q: How do I immediately block a lost or stolen card?
**A:** Tap Block Lost Card and confirm the last 4 digits, SMS BLOCK <last 4 digits> to 567676 from your registered mobile, or call 1800 1234 (24x7) and press 0 then 1.
*(Category: Card Security)*

### Q: Can financial transactions be made through WhatsApp?
**A:** No. This assistant helps with information, eligibility, and service requests only. Never share OTPs, PINs, CVV or passwords here.
*(Category: Security Guardrail)*

### Q: What documents are required to open a savings account?
**A:** Aadhaar, PAN, and a photograph. Eligible customers can often complete Video KYC online via YONO SBI.
*(Category: Account Opening)*

### Q: How do I submit an insurance claim?
**A:** Use Insurance & Claims for the checklist and document guidance. SBI Life claims line: 1800 267 9090. Keep policy number and hospital bills ready.
*(Category: Claims)*

### Q: How can I find my IFSC and branch details?
**A:** Share your city or PIN, or use Locate Branch & ATM. IFSC is also printed on the passbook and cheque book.
*(Category: Branch Info)*

### Q: What is the minimum balance for a savings account?
**A:** NIL. SBI waived the Average Monthly Balance requirement on savings accounts from 11 March 2020. Charges for specific account types may apply as per the schedule of charges.
*(Category: Accounts)*

### Q: How do I activate net banking or mobile banking?
**A:** Register on OnlineSBI / YONO SBI using your debit card or at the branch. The chatbot will never ask for your password.
*(Category: Digital Banking)*

### Q: What should I do if I see an unauthorized transaction?
**A:** Block the card immediately, then report it: 1800 11 1109 (toll-free), 94491 12211 or 080-2659 9990. Keep SMS alerts as evidence and raise a dispute ticket.
*(Category: Fraud)*

### Q: Are senior citizens eligible for higher FD rates?
**A:** Yes - senior citizens get an additional 0.50% p.a.; for example 6.95% on the 444-day Amrit Vrishti deposit versus 6.45% for others.
*(Category: Deposits)*

### Q: How long does home loan sanction take?
**A:** It depends on complete documents, CIBIL score and property verification. The assistant never promises a sanction date.
*(Category: Loans)*

### Q: Can I update my registered mobile number?
**A:** Visit your home branch with KYC or use authenticated channels. WhatsApp cannot change registered numbers alone.
*(Category: Profile)*

### Q: What are branch working hours?
**A:** Typically 10:00 AM - 04:00 PM on weekdays; branches are closed on Sundays and 2nd/4th Saturdays. Confirm for your branch as timings can vary.
*(Category: Branch Info)*

### Q: Do you offer nomination facilities?
**A:** Yes. Nomination can be registered for savings, deposits and lockers; check status via 1800 1234 or net banking/YONO.
*(Category: Accounts)*

### Q: How do I download account statements?
**A:** Use OnlineSBI / YONO SBI for PDF statements, or missed call 09223866666 for a mini statement. The assistant explains steps but cannot email statements without authenticated channels.
*(Category: Statements)*
